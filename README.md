Overview & Purpose

This project focuses on analyzing hospitality and hotel booking data to generate meaningful business insights related to revenue performance, occupancy trends, and customer behavior.

The goal is to help stakeholders such as hotel managers, revenue teams, and analysts understand how business performance changes over time and identify opportunities to improve:

Revenue generation
Occupancy optimization
Pricing strategy
Booking efficiency
Seasonal demand planning
📌 Key Objectives
Analyze hotel revenue performance over time
Identify seasonal demand and occupancy trends
Evaluate booking vs cancellation patterns
Understand property-wise and category-wise performance
Support data-driven revenue optimization decisions
🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
📊 Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and initial analysis
🔄 Power Query – Data transformation and ETL process
📈 Power BI – Interactive dashboards and visualization
📊 Key Features
Cleaned and structured hospitality booking data using Excel & Power Query
Built KPI-driven Power BI dashboards for revenue analysis
Analyzed occupancy rates across time periods
Identified seasonal fluctuations in demand
Evaluated booking vs cancellation trends
Created visual insights for revenue optimization
📈 Dashboard Insights
Total revenue performance tracking
Occupancy rate analysis by time and property
Seasonal demand trends (monthly/weekly patterns)
Booking vs cancellation ratio
Revenue contribution by property/type/category
Peak and low demand periods identification
🚀 Business Impact
Improved visibility into hotel revenue performance
Helped identify seasonal demand patterns
Supported pricing and occupancy optimization decisions
Reduced manual reporting effort through automation
Enabled faster, data-driven decision-making
